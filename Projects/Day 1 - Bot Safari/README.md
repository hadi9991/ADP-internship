# Task 1 — Bot Safari 🤖

**Time:** ~40 minutes · **Due:** before Day 2 · **Tools needed:** none, just your phone or laptop

---

## The idea

Today you learned what a chatbot is, how it works, and how it differs from an IVR. Now go use two of them properly and tell us what you found.

You are not reviewing them as a customer. You are looking at them as the person who will build the next one.

---

## What to do

### 1. Pick two chatbots

Choose **two bots from two different industries** — for example one telecom and one bank, or one airline and one online shop.

Some starting points:

- [We chatbot](https://te.eg/wps/portal/te/Personal) — telecom, web
- [Elm WhatsApp bot](https://wa.me/966920000356) — government services, WhatsApp
- Any bank, airline, delivery or e-commerce bot you already use

### 2. Give each bot a real task

Not `hi` and not `what can you do`. Pick something you actually want an answer to:

- How much is my bill?
- Where is my order?
- What are the working hours of the nearest branch?
- How do I renew my subscription?

Then **try to break it**. Halfway through, type something it will not expect — a typo, a different language, a completely unrelated question. Watch what it does.

### 3. Write it up

One page per bot, using the template below.

---

## Template — fill this in for each bot

**Bot:** _company name_
**Channel:** _web / mobile app / WhatsApp / Facebook / other_
**The task I tried:** _one sentence_

**The conversation:**

> Paste the conversation here, or attach screenshots.

**Did it work?**

- Did you get the answer you wanted? _yes / no / partly_
- How many messages did it take? _number_
- How long did the whole thing take? _minutes_

**What kind of bot was it?**

- Rule-based, AI-powered, or hybrid? _your guess_
- What gave it away? _e.g. it only accepted the exact buttons it showed me / it understood me when I rephrased_

**When I typed something unexpected:**

- What did it do? _repeated the same menu / apologized and re-asked / understood anyway / offered a human_
- Was that a good recovery? _yes / no, and why_

**The escape hatch:**

- Did it offer to connect you to a human? _yes / no_
- If yes — how easy was it to reach? If no — how did that feel?

**One thing I would change:** _one or two sentences_

---

## The closing question ⭐

This one matters more than the rest. After both write-ups, answer:

> **For the task you completed, would an IVR have been better or worse than the chatbot? Why?**
>
> Write three sentences. Think about the channel, the input, and what you needed to see or hear.

Keep your answer. On Day 2 you will call two real IVRs, and we will ask you the same question again — your answer will probably change, and *that* is the interesting part.

---

## Rules

- ✅ Real conversations only. Screenshots or pasted text, not summaries from memory.
- ✅ Two different industries. Two banking bots teach you half as much.
- ❌ Do not just say hello and stop. A bot that never got a real question tells you nothing.
- 🤔 If a bot solved your task in two messages, that is a finding too — write down *why* it was so easy.

---

## How to submit

Put your write-up in this folder as `your-name.md` (or a PDF / Word file if you prefer), including the screenshots, and send it before Day 2 starts.

---

## What we are looking for

We are not grading your writing. We are looking for whether you noticed:

- the difference between a bot that **understands** and a bot that **matches keywords**
- what happens at the edges, when the conversation leaves the happy path
- whether there was a way out to a human, and how hard it was to find

Those three things are the whole job.
